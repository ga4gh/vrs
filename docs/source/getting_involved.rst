Getting Involved
@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@

VRS is driven by community involvement. This product is early in development, and we need your input and expertise to help make this as effective as possible. Here are a few ways that you
can get involved:


* `Join the group through GA4GH <https://www.ga4gh.org/get-involved/join-our-community/join/>`_ to receive regular updates on our progress.

* Join our monthly meetings. The schedule for upcoming meetings can be found `on the product website in GA4GH <https://www.ga4gh.org/product/variation-representation/>`_.

    * Scroll to the bottom and click on "Meetings".

* Join the GA4GH VRS Slack channel to connect with the community.

* Participate in the GA4GH Genomic Knowledge Standards (GKS) Work Stream Think Tank meetings, where VRS issues and specification topics are often discussed. Information about GKS and participation opportunities is available on the `Genomic Knowledge Standards Work Stream page <https://www.ga4gh.org/work_stream/genomic-knowledge-standards/>`_.

* Read the machine readable schema definitions, participate in Discussions, and raise Issues in the `VRS GitHub repository <https://github.com/ga4gh/vrs>`_.
